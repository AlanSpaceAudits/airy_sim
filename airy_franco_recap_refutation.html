Special PleadingArgument from IgnoranceSelf-Sealing Argument

Relativity is not correct by fiat. It has to make mutually exclusive predictions about the dynamics.

A physical theory earns the word "correct" by predicting one outcome for a moving Earth and a different outcome for a stationary one, then matching the measurement. If it predicts the same reading, 20.55″, in every frame no matter how the Earth moves, it has made no mutually exclusive prediction about the dynamics of relative motion, and Airy cannot test it at all. A claim no result could contradict is not a strong claim. It is a self-sealing one.

Take relativity as it has to be taken, as a real dynamical claim, and it does split. For a genuinely moving Earth the water carries the 30 km/s, and in that frame special relativity reduces to the transverse Fresnel drag Franco wrote himself: internal angle β(n−1/n) = 11.88″, reading 15.80″. That is the mutually exclusive prediction. Airy's plate returned 20.55″, the stationary-Earth value. The moving branch is falsified; the theory keeps its footing here only by retreating to "every frame reads the same," which is the retreat into unfalsifiability.

This is why the framework is Lorentz's and not something newer. Lorentz's electron theory is mathematically the same transformation, and it derives the identical null from the Earth being at rest in the ether. Einstein kept the transformation and dropped the ether's name; the postulates are still postulates and still have to be tested. "No conceivable mechanism for water to do anything but refract" is the ether-at-rest prediction restated: it expects a null because it was built to expect one, and the null is exactly what a stationary Earth gives.

And he cannot have it both ways: the same theory cannot predict the null for Airy and reduce to classical transverse drag for Fizeau and Jones at once. The drag is real, measured by Fizeau and Jones; the only thing that turns it off for Airy is the choice to compute in the Earth-rest frame.

Why the AU and the 30 km/s "agree" with aberration

On the Space the question came up: why do the astronomical unit and the 30 km/s orbital speed come out consistent with aberration? They come out consistent because aberration is where several of them were fixed. The 30 km/s is not an independent measurement. It is v⊕ = c·tan κ ≈ 29.8 km/s, read straight off the aberration constant κ and c. The AU follows as AU = v⊕·T/2π ≈ 149.5 million km, the same figure the parallax route reaches through AU = R⊕/sin π⊕.

These are not separate pillars that happen to line up. The aberration constant κ, the solar parallax π⊕, c, the AU, the Earth's radius and the 30 km/s all sit in one ring identity, κ = 2πR⊕ / (c·T·sin π⊕), so each is defined through the others. Laplace asked Delambre to re-derive the aberration constant from Io's eclipses alone; it returned Bradley's value, and Laplace called it "a perfect agreement between two results deduced from entirely different methods." Both methods were anchored in aberration. de Sitter and Brouwer later put it plainly: the constants "are, of course, dependent on each other through observations."

Agreement inside a system whose constants were fixed by that system is bookkeeping, not confirmation. It is the same shape as Newton's G, which enters only as a ratio, G = 3g/(4πR⊕ρ⊕): scale every length in the solar system by one factor and the two independent determinations of the Earth's mass still agree at every scale, fixing no absolute size. Internal consistency is a property of the ledger, not proof that the Earth moves.

"So you think every physicist for four centuries is wrong?"

No. Heliocentrism's correctness rests on Kepler's kinematics and Newton's dynamics, and those two are inter-derivable: each recovers the other, and neither reaches deeper than the other into the real motions of the bodies. What they build together is a set of ratio-based, mutually-reliant constants that is internally consistent, and a geocentric assignment of the same ratios is equally consistent. Airy's null is the datum that fits the second reading. Rejecting one interpretation of one experiment is not "all of physics is wrong."

"Hundreds of thousands of physicists, the finest minds, would have to be wrong" is an appeal to numbers and to authority, not to the plate Airy exposed. And note the move on the hypotheses: the Popperian test, in which H₀ is the statement you try to falsify, has been turned into a show of hands, "I vote H₀." H₀ is not voted on. It is falsified or it survives a test. Airy's water telescope was the test, and it returned the stationary-Earth value.

Every superseded framework, Ptolemy's epicycles, Newton's absolute space, could have pointed to its centuries of success and its consensus of great minds. Longevity is not the judge. Experimentum summus judex.
